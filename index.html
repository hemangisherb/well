Wake Me Up Green Essence

A bright green morning blend, straight from the Lose Weight chapter. If you've got spinach, a banana and an orange, you can make it today.

You'll need

  • A small handful of fresh oregano
  • 1 frozen banana
  • 1 handful of spinach
  • 2 large kale leaves, without stem
  • 1 peeled orange
  • ⅓ cup soaked walnuts
  • 1 cup filtered water

Make it

  1. Soak the walnuts overnight in filtered water, then drain and rinse.
  2. Pour in the water first, add the greens and soft ingredients, and blend 20–30 seconds until the greens vanish.
  3. Add the frozen banana and the rest. Blend until thick and creamy, pausing once to push the mixture down.
  4. Taste, then adjust: more water if too thick, more frozen fruit if too thin. Pour and enjoy straight away.

Swap: no walnuts? Use soaked cashews or a spoon of nut butter. Every recipe in the book carries a swap like this one.
