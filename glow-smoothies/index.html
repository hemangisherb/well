Frequently asked questions

What smoothie is good for glowing skin?

The best smoothies for glowing skin combine vitamin C–rich fruit (berries, citrus, mango) with healthy fats (avocado, nuts, seeds) and hydrating greens or cucumber. Our 26 smoothies for glowing skin were designed around exactly that trio.

Do smoothies help your skin?

They can. Skin reflects nutrition: adequate vitamin C supports collagen, healthy fats support the skin barrier, and hydration plus antioxidants help fight dullness. A daily glass from our smoothies for glowing skin collection is an easy, enjoyable way to feed your skin.

What should I drink for clear skin?

Water first — then nutrient-dense smoothies. Avoid loading them with added sugar, which can work against clear skin. Every one of our smoothies for glowing skin uses whole fruit and no refined sugar.

How long until I see results?

Skin turns over roughly every 28 days, so give any nutrition change at least a month of consistency. With 26 smoothies for glowing skin to rotate through, staying consistent is the delicious part.
