Goji Berry Almond Puree

Ingredients

  • 1 cup almond milk
  • ⅓ cup goji berries
  • ½ to 1 cups frozen raspberries
  • 1 scoop dairy-free protein powder
  • 1 tbsp almond butter

Method

  1. Pour in the liquid first, add the greens and soft fresh ingredients, and blend 20–30 seconds until the greens vanish.
  2. Add the frozen fruit, powders, seeds and the rest. Blend until thick and creamy, pausing once to push the mixture down.
  3. Taste, then adjust: more liquid if too thick, more frozen fruit if too thin. Pour and enjoy straight away.

5 minutes · 1 large glass · 1 blender

Why these smoothies for glowing skin work

Skin reflects what's happening inside your body, and these smoothies for glowing skin target the three pillars of radiance. Collagen support: vitamin C from berries and citrus is essential for your body to produce collagen — the protein that keeps skin firm. Antioxidant defence: colourful berries and dark leafy greens neutralise the free radicals that cause premature ageing. Deep hydration and healthy fats: avocado, coconut, and water-rich fruit keep skin cells plump and supple.

Drink one daily for two weeks and watch your mirror. The glow from consistent nutrition beats any topical product — because it's coming from healthy cells, not surface coverage. Every recipe includes exact quantities and an easy ingredient swap.

Frequently asked questions

What smoothie is best for glowing skin?

Berry-avocado smoothies are best — berries provide vitamin C and antioxidants for collagen, avocado provides healthy fats that nourish skin cells. Our Goji Berry Almond Puree below is a favourite.

Do smoothies really improve skin?

Yes — consistent intake of vitamin C, antioxidants, and healthy fats visibly improves skin texture and radiance within 2–3 weeks. It's one of the most evidence-backed beauty habits.

What ingredients are best for skin glow?

Berries (vitamin C, antioxidants), avocado (healthy fats), citrus (collagen support), mango (beta-carotene), and cucumber (hydration). Our 26 glow recipes rotate through all of these.

How long until I see results?

Most people notice brighter, more hydrated skin within 10–14 days of daily smoothies. Deeper changes in texture take 4–6 weeks of consistency.
