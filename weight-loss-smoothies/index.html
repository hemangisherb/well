Frequently asked questions

What is the best smoothie for weight loss?

The best smoothie for weight loss is one high in fibre and protein but modest in calories — think leafy greens, berries, half an avocado or a spoon of nut butter, blended with water or unsweetened nut milk. Our low calorie smoothie recipes chapter has 18 such blends, each designed to keep you full without the calorie load.

Can smoothies really help you lose weight?

Yes — when they replace a higher-calorie meal or snack. A well-built smoothie delivers vitamins, fibre and hydration for 200–300 calories, which makes portion control almost effortless. The 18 low calorie smoothie recipes in this chapter were created exactly for that job.

How many calories should a weight-loss smoothie have?

Aim for roughly 200–350 calories per serving, depending on whether it replaces a snack or a meal. Every recipe in The Ultimate Smoothie Wellness Guide lists its ingredients with exact quantities, so you always know what is in your glass.

Are fruit smoothies OK on a diet?

Absolutely — whole fruit brings fibre that slows sugar absorption and keeps you satisfied. The trick is balance: pair fruit with greens, healthy fats or protein. That is the formula behind every one of our low calorie smoothie recipes.
