Wake Me Up Green Essence

Ingredients

  • A small handful fresh oregano
  • 1 frozen banana
  • 1 handful spinach
  • 2 large leaves of kale, without stem
  • 1 peeled orange
  • ⅓ cup soaked walnuts
  • 1 cup filtered water

Method

  1. Soak-ahead: soak the nuts or seeds listed overnight in filtered water, then drain and rinse.
  2. Pour in the liquid first, add the greens and soft fresh ingredients, and blend 20–30 seconds until the greens vanish.
  3. Add the frozen fruit, powders, seeds and the rest. Blend until thick and creamy, pausing once to push the mixture down.
  4. Taste, then adjust: more liquid if too thick, more frozen fruit if too thin. Pour and enjoy straight away.

5 minutes · 1 large glass · 1 blender

Why these low calorie smoothie recipes work

These low calorie smoothie recipes work because they follow three simple principles. First, fibre — from leafy greens, berries, and chia — slows digestion and keeps you feeling full. Second, volume — water-rich ingredients like cucumber and citrus make each glass feel substantial without adding calories. Third, balance — a small amount of healthy fat from nuts or avocado prevents the blood-sugar spike and crash that leads to cravings.

You don't need to skip meals or go hungry. Replace one meal or snack a day with one of these blends, pair it with balanced eating and regular movement, and let steady habits do the quiet work over time. Each recipe includes exact quantities and a smart swap, so you always blend with what you already have.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best smoothie for weight loss?

The best smoothie for weight loss is high in fibre and protein but modest in calories — think leafy greens, berries, half an avocado or a spoon of nut butter, blended with water or unsweetened nut milk. All 18 recipes in our Lose Weight chapter follow this formula.

Can smoothies really help you lose weight?

Yes, when they replace a higher-calorie meal or snack. A well-built smoothie delivers vitamins, fibre and hydration for 200–350 calories, making portion control almost effortless.

How many calories should a weight-loss smoothie have?

Aim for roughly 200–350 calories per serving, depending on whether it replaces a snack or a full meal. Every recipe in The Ultimate Smoothie Wellness Guide uses exact quantities so you always know what's in your glass.

Are fruit smoothies OK on a diet?

Absolutely — whole fruit brings fibre that slows sugar absorption and keeps you satisfied. The trick is balance: pair fruit with greens, healthy fats or protein, which is the formula behind every recipe here.

When should I drink a weight-loss smoothie?

Most people replace breakfast or lunch. The fibre and healthy fats keep you full for 3–4 hours, making it easier to eat less at your next meal without feeling deprived.
