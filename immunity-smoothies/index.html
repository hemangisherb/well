Frequently asked questions

What smoothie is best for immunity?

Look for blends rich in vitamin C (citrus, berries, kiwi), ginger or turmeric for their anti-inflammatory compounds, and leafy greens for folate and iron. Our 12 immune boosting smoothie recipes combine these ingredients in tasty, balanced ways.

Can smoothies boost your immune system?

No single food “can ‘boost’ immunity overnight, but a consistent supply of vitamins, minerals and antioxidants genuinely supports immune function. A daily smoothie from our immune boosting smoothie recipes is one of the easiest ways to keep that supply steady.

Is ginger good in smoothies for immunity?

Yes — ginger contains gingerol, a compound studied for anti-inflammatory and antioxidant effects, and it adds a warming zing that pairs beautifully with citrus and pineapple. Several of our immune boosting smoothie recipes feature it.
