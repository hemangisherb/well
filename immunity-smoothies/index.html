Drink Your Salad

Ingredients

  • 1 cup filtered water
  • 6 ice cubes
  • ½ fresh avocado
  • 1 medium tomato, Roma or Heirloom
  • 1–2 cloves of garlic
  • 1 small handful mint
  • 2 broccoli florets
  • 1 handful fresh parsley
  • ½ green bell pepper, no seeds
  • ½ lime, juice squeezed into blender

Method

  1. Pour in the liquid first, add the greens and soft fresh ingredients, and blend 20–30 seconds until the greens vanish.
  2. Add the vegetables, herbs and seasonings. Blend until smooth, then taste and add more lemon, lime or spice if needed.
  3. Taste, then adjust: more liquid if too thick, more frozen fruit if too thin. Pour and enjoy straight away.

5 minutes · 1 large glass · 1 blender

Romaine Ginger Potency

Ingredients

  • 1 frozen peeled avocado
  • A small chunk of fresh peeled ginger
  • 1 fresh peeled kiwi
  • 5–6 leaves of romaine lettuce
  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • Optional addition:
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds

Method

  1. Pour in the liquid first, add the greens and soft fresh ingredients, and blend 20–30 seconds until the greens vanish.
  2. Add the frozen fruit, powders, seeds and the rest. Blend until thick and creamy, pausing once to push the mixture down.
  3. Taste, then adjust: more liquid if too thick, more frozen fruit if too thin. Pour and enjoy straight away.

5 minutes · 1 large glass · 1 blender

Why these immune boosting smoothie recipes work

Your immune system runs on nutrients, and these immune boosting smoothie recipes deliver them in the most absorbable form — whole food. Vitamin C from pineapple, citrus, and berries supports white blood cell function. Ginger and turmeric are time-tested anti-inflammatories. Leafy greens provide folate and iron. And because everything is blended, your body absorbs these nutrients faster than from whole fruit alone.

One glass a day during cold season is one of the simplest health habits you can build. The recipes are designed to taste like treats, not medicine — so you'll actually look forward to them. Each includes exact quantities and a smart ingredient swap.

Frequently asked questions

What smoothie is best for immunity?

Citrus and pineapple-based smoothies with ginger are best — they're highest in vitamin C and anti-inflammatory compounds. Our Pineapple Kale Explosion below is a top pick.

Can smoothies boost your immune system?

They provide concentrated vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants that support immune function. No single food prevents illness, but daily nutrient-dense smoothies are one of the best dietary habits for resilience.

What ingredients boost immunity in smoothies?

Vitamin C (citrus, pineapple, berries), ginger, turmeric, leafy greens, and honey. All 12 recipes in our Immunity chapter combine several of these.

Should I drink immunity smoothies every day?

Yes — consistency matters more than any single ingredient. One glass daily, especially in colder months, keeps your nutrient levels steady.
