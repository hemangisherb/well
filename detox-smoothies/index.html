Frequently asked questions

What should I put in a detox smoothie?

Start with leafy greens (spinach or kale), add cucumber or celery for hydration, brighten it with lemon or lime, and sweeten naturally with green apple or pineapple. Fresh mint or ginger adds a cleansing kick. Our 23 green detox smoothie recipes use exactly this blueprint in delicious combinations.

Do detox smoothies actually work?

Your liver and kidneys do the real detoxing — but green smoothies genuinely help by flooding your body with water, fibre, chlorophyll and antioxidants while crowding out processed food. Think of these green detox smoothie recipes as delicious support for the organs already doing the job.

Can I drink a detox smoothie every day?

Yes. A daily green smoothie is one of the simplest healthy habits you can build — most people have one for breakfast. With 23 green detox smoothie recipes to rotate through, you will never get bored.

What is the best time to drink a detox smoothie?

Morning is ideal: it hydrates you after sleep and sets a healthy tone for the day. Many readers also enjoy one as an afternoon pick-me-up instead of coffee. Each recipe takes just five minutes, so either slot works.
