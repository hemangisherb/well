Green Mango Chill

Ingredients

  • 1 cup filtered water
  • 1 cup frozen mango
  • 1 medium frozen banana
  • 2 cups chopped celery
  • 1 handful of parsley

Method

  1. Pour in the liquid first, add the greens and soft fresh ingredients, and blend 20–30 seconds until the greens vanish.
  2. Add the frozen fruit, powders, seeds and the rest. Blend until thick and creamy, pausing once to push the mixture down.
  3. Taste, then adjust: more liquid if too thick, more frozen fruit if too thin. Pour and enjoy straight away.

5 minutes · 1 large glass · 1 blender

Why these green detox smoothie recipes work

Your body already has a detox system — your liver, kidneys, and digestive tract work around the clock. These green detox smoothie recipes support that system by flooding it with what it needs: hydration from cucumber and coconut water, fibre from greens and fruit to keep things moving, antioxidants from berries and leafy greens to neutralise free radicals, and ginger and lemon to support digestion.

There's no fasting, no juice-only days, no misery. Just replace your morning drink with one of these green blends for a week and notice the difference in your energy, skin, and digestion. Each recipe takes five minutes and includes a smart swap for ingredients you don't have.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best green detox smoothie?

The best green detox smoothie combines leafy greens (spinach or kale), a hydrating base (coconut water or filtered water), cleansing fruit (green apple, pineapple, or lemon), and ginger. Our Green Mango Chill below is a perfect example.

Do detox smoothies actually work?

They support your body's natural detox processes — liver, kidneys, and gut — by providing hydration, fibre, and antioxidants. They're not magic, but replacing a sugary drink with a green smoothie every day makes a real difference.

How often should I drink a detox smoothie?

Daily is ideal, especially in the morning. The 23 recipes in our Detoxify chapter give you enough variety for three weeks without repeating.

Can I make detox smoothies ahead of time?

Yes — blend and refrigerate for up to 24 hours. Give it a good shake before drinking. For best nutrition, drink within a few hours of blending.

What greens are best for detox smoothies?

Spinach is the mildest (you won't taste it), kale is more nutrient-dense, and chard adds minerals. All three appear across our detox recipes — start with spinach if you're new to green smoothies.
