1. Green Mango Chill (Full Free Recipe)

5 MINUTES · 1 LARGE GLASS · 1 BLENDER

This one leads with mango and banana, with celery and parsley folded in so gently you notice the flavour long before the greens. Light, hydrating and clean-tasting, it is the kind of blend you reach for when you want to feel refreshed rather than weighed down.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup filtered water
  • 1 cup frozen mango
  • 1 medium frozen banana
  • 2 cups chopped celery
  • 1 handful of parsley

Method

  1. Pour in the liquid first, add the greens and soft fresh ingredients, and blend 20–30 seconds until the greens vanish.
  2. Add the frozen fruit, powders, seeds and the rest. Blend until thick and creamy, pausing once to push the mixture down.
  3. Taste, then adjust: more liquid if too thick, more frozen fruit if too thin. Pour and enjoy straight away.

Why Green Detox Smoothies Work

Green detox smoothie recipes work because they solve two problems at once: hydration and micronutrient intake. Most people are mildly dehydrated and under-eating vegetables — a single green smoothie delivers 2–3 cups of produce plus 300–400ml of fluid in a form that takes five minutes to make and two minutes to drink.

The "detox" angle is real but indirect. Your liver and kidneys do the actual detoxifying; what green smoothies provide is the raw material those organs need — water for flushing, fibre for gut health, vitamin C and antioxidants for cellular repair, potassium for fluid balance. Celery brings natural sodium and hydration; parsley is rich in vitamin K and chlorophyll; mango adds vitamin A and natural sweetness that makes the whole thing drinkable.

The key insight from our recipe testing: the fruit-to-greens ratio determines whether someone makes a green smoothie once or makes it a habit. At 60% fruit or higher, the greens become nutritionally present but flavour-absent — and that is what turns a "health chore" into a daily craving.

Tips & Variations

Rotate your greens: Do not use spinach every day. Alternate between spinach, kale, celery, parsley, romaine and chard across the week — different greens have different nutrient profiles, and rotation prevents oxalate buildup.

Always use frozen fruit: Frozen mango, banana or pineapple gives thickness without ice (which waters down flavour). Keep bags in the freezer and you are always two minutes from a smoothie.

Add healthy fat for staying power: A tablespoon of chia seeds, flaxseed or half an avocado turns a light detox smoothie into something that holds you for hours.

Prep ahead: Portion greens + fruit into freezer bags on Sunday. Each morning: empty bag into blender, add water, blend. The fastest healthy breakfast that exists.

FAQ

What is the best green detox smoothie recipe?

The best green detox smoothie balances hydrating vegetables with enough fruit to taste great. Our Green Mango Chill — mango, banana, celery and parsley — is a perfect starter: light, clean-tasting and genuinely refreshing.

Do detox smoothies actually detox your body?

Your liver and kidneys handle detoxification — but green detox smoothies support them with hydration, fibre, antioxidants and vitamins. They are a practical way to flood your body with the nutrients those organs need to work well.

Can I drink a green detox smoothie every day?

Yes — a daily green smoothie is one of the easiest healthy habits to maintain. Rotate your greens (spinach, kale, celery, parsley) across the week for a broader range of nutrients.

How do you make green smoothies taste good?

The secret is ratio: at least 60% fruit to 40% greens, plus frozen fruit for thickness. Mango, banana and pineapple are the best fruits for masking green flavours — which is exactly why Green Mango Chill works so well.
