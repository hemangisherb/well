Apple Banana Smoothie Recipe (Simple & Sweet)

An apple banana smoothie is the simplest good idea in the whole smoothie world: two fruits you almost always have at home, five minutes, one blender, and a glass that tastes like a treat. This version comes from The Ultimate Smoothie Wellness Guide — recipe № 30, Brave Dandelion Buzz, from the Lose Weight chapter — and it pairs the apple and banana with dandelion greens and hemp seeds, so the sweet fruit does the flavour work while the greens quietly do the nourishing.

It is a genuinely simple recipe: five ingredients, three steps, and about 305 calories a glass. The frozen banana makes it thick and cold like a milkshake, the apple adds a fresh, crunch-sweet lift, and the dandelion brings vitamin K, vitamin A and a proper serving of leafy greens. If green smoothies have ever felt like a chore, this is the one to start with — the book says it plainly: banana leads, and the dandelion blends in without taking over.

The Recipe

5 MINUTES · 1 LARGE GLASS · 1 BLENDER

If you like your smoothies fruity rather than grassy, start here: banana leads, and dandelion blends in without taking over. Unapologetically green, this is for days when you want a full serving of leafy flavour and do not mind tasting it.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups fresh dandelions
  • 1 frozen banana
  • 1 medium size apple
  • 1 tbsp hemp seeds
  • 1 cup filtered water

Optional boost: a small chunk of ginger.

Method

  1. Pour in the liquid first, add the greens and soft fresh ingredients, and blend 20–30 seconds until the greens vanish.
  2. Add the frozen fruit, powders, seeds and the rest. Blend until thick and creamy, pausing once to push the mixture down.
  3. Taste, then adjust: more liquid if too thick, more frozen fruit if too thin. Pour and enjoy straight away.
Nutrition per glass (estimated): ~305 cal · Protein 8g · Carbs 63g · Fibre 12g · Fat 6g · Natural sugars ~34g
High in vitamin K · High in vitamin A · Omega-3 fats from seeds or nuts

Why It Works

Apple and banana are a natural pair because they do different jobs. The banana brings the body: potassium, quick natural carbohydrate for energy, and — once frozen — the thick, ice-cream texture that makes a smoothie feel like a meal instead of a juice. The apple brings freshness and fibre, including pectin, the soluble fibre that helps you feel full and supports steady digestion. Together they are sweet enough that this blend needs no honey, no syrup and no added sugar at all.

The greens are the reason this is a weight-loss recipe rather than just a fruit smoothie. Two cups of dandelions add vitamin K and vitamin A for almost no calories, and the 12g of fibre across the whole glass is what keeps you satisfied for hours — fibre slows everything down, so the fruit sugars release steadily instead of spiking and crashing. The tablespoon of hemp seeds finishes the job with omega-3 fats and a little extra protein, rounding the glass out into a light breakfast you can actually rely on.

At roughly 305 calories it sits exactly where a light meal should: enough to count as breakfast, light enough to leave you feeling clear rather than heavy. Drink it first thing, or as a mid-morning replacement for a snack that would have cost you twice the calories.

Tips & Variations

New to dandelion? Start with one cup instead of two and make up the difference with spinach, which vanishes into the fruit completely. Work up to the full two cups over a few days — dandelion has a pleasantly bitter, herbal edge, and your taste buds adjust fast once the banana and apple are doing the talking.

Freeze the banana properly: Peel ripe bananas, break them in halves and freeze them flat in a bag. A frozen banana is what makes this thick and cold; a fresh one will come out thin and warm, and no amount of ice quite fixes it. Keep a bag of frozen banana halves in the freezer and this becomes a two-minute breakfast.

Add the ginger boost: The book suggests a small chunk of fresh ginger, and it is worth trying — it adds a warm bite that balances the sweetness and makes the whole glass feel brighter. Peel it first, and start small: a piece the size of your thumbnail is plenty.

Make it sweeter without sugar: Use the ripest banana you have — the ones with brown spots are sweetest — and pick a sweet apple variety like Gala or Fuji rather than a tart Granny Smith. The riper the fruit, the less you will ever miss added sweetness.

Turn it into a protein breakfast: Add a scoop of protein powder or a spoonful of Greek yoghurt with the frozen fruit. It thickens the smoothie slightly, so add a splash more water, and it turns a light 305-calorie glass into a proper post-workout meal.

FAQ

Does this apple banana smoothie taste like dandelion?

You will notice the greens — the book describes this blend as unapologetically green — but the banana leads and the apple sweetens, so it tastes fruity first and leafy second. If you want the greens to disappear completely, swap the dandelions for a big handful of spinach.

Can I make this apple banana smoothie without dandelions?

Yes. Use a big handful of fresh spinach or a cup of kale instead and the smoothie stays sweet, thick and green, just milder. The banana and apple carry the flavour either way, so the swap changes the greens, not the character of the drink.

Is an apple banana smoothie good for weight loss?

This one is a genuine light meal at around 305 calories, with 12g of fibre and no added sugar — the sweetness comes only from the fruit. The fibre and the water content keep you full, which is what makes it useful for weight loss: it replaces a heavier breakfast rather than adding to it.

Can I use a fresh banana instead of a frozen one?

You can, but the texture changes. A frozen banana is what makes this thick and cold like a milkshake. With a fresh banana, add a small handful of ice cubes and use a cold apple from the fridge so the smoothie still comes out chilled and creamy rather than thin and warm.
