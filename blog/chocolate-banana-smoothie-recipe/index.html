Chocolate Banana Smoothie Recipe (Healthy & Indulgent)

If a chocolate banana smoothie is your idea of heaven, but the café version is a sugar bomb in disguise, this healthy take changes everything. Frozen banana, almond butter and raw cacao blend thick, creamy and chocolatey, with blueberries, figs and hidden spinach for fibre.

This is The Great Fig Goddess from the Energy & Strength chapter of The Ultimate Smoothie Wellness Guide — plus raw cacao, which the book’s add-in guide pairs with banana and nut butter.

The Recipe: The Great Fig Goddess

5 MINUTES · 1 LARGE GLASS · 1 BLENDER

If you like your smoothies fruity rather than grassy, start here: banana and blueberries lead, and spinach blends in without taking over. Made to keep you full — the creamy base, fibre and healthy fats work as a proper breakfast or a satisfying lunch.

Ingredients

  • 1–1½ cup almond milk
  • 1 frozen banana
  • 1 tbsp almond butter
  • ¼ cup frozen blueberries
  • 1 cup fresh spinach
  • 4 frozen figs
  • 1–2 tbsp raw cacao (optional — this makes it the chocolate banana version)

Method

  1. Pour in the liquid first, add the greens and soft fresh ingredients, and blend 20–30 seconds until the greens vanish.
  2. Add the frozen fruit, powders, seeds and the rest. Blend until thick and creamy, pausing once to push the mixture down.
  3. Taste, then adjust: more liquid if too thick, more frozen fruit if too thin. Pour and enjoy straight away.

Why It Works

This chocolate banana smoothie works because every ingredient pulls in the same direction. Raw cacao delivers genuine deep-chocolate flavour plus plant compounds, with no added sugar — the difference between a milkshake and a smoothie that happens to taste like one. This pairing is the trick the whole recipe is built on.

Banana brings fast carbs and natural sweetness; almond butter’s healthy fats and protein slow digestion, so energy arrives steadily, not in one spike and crash.

The cup of spinach is the quiet hero: rich in vitamin K, vitamin A and folate, yet its mild flavour vanishes behind the banana, berries and cacao.

With 11g of fibre per glass from the figs, banana and blueberries, this blend genuinely satisfies at roughly 380 calories — a real breakfast or lunch replacement, with milkshake texture from frozen fruit, no dairy. The figs and blueberries bring all the sweetness — nothing added.

Tips & Variations

Make it thicker: Freeze the banana solid and add the almond milk slowly — thicker blends eat like soft-serve.

Boost the protein: Add the book’s optional boost — protein powder or hemp seeds — for 15g+ protein and real post-workout recovery. This turns the smoothie into a genuine meal after training.

No figs? Two pitted dates give the same honeyed sweetness and thick texture. And if raw cacao feels too bitter at first, start with 1 tbsp and work up — 2 tbsp is for serious chocolate people.

Dairy-free by design: Almond milk keeps it light, but oat milk is the creamiest swap if you want maximum indulgence — still fully plant-based either way, so it suits vegan readers too.

Meal prep: Bag the frozen fruit, spinach and cacao in portions; in the morning, tip one into the blender with almond milk and almond butter — breakfast in under two minutes, with no measuring.

FAQ

Is this smoothie actually healthy?

Yes — no added sugar; the chocolate flavour comes from raw cacao, and the base is banana, blueberries, figs, spinach and almond milk. Around 380 calories with 11g of fibre.

Can you taste the spinach in this smoothie?

Not at all — banana, blueberries and cacao completely overpower the spinach flavour.

Can I make it without almond butter?

Yes — swap in peanut butter, or use half a frozen avocado for creaminess.
