Nutty Berry Prospect

Ingredients

  • 1 large frozen banana
  • 3 large frozen strawberries
  • ½ cup frozen blueberries
  • 2 cups fresh baby spinach
  • 1 cup filtered water
  • 1 tbsp peanut butter

Method

  1. Pour in the liquid first, add the greens and soft fresh ingredients, and blend 20–30 seconds until the greens vanish.
  2. Add the frozen fruit, powders, seeds and the rest. Blend until thick and creamy, pausing once to push the mixture down.
  3. Taste, then adjust: more liquid if too thick, more frozen fruit if too thin. Pour and enjoy straight away.

5 minutes · 1 large glass · 1 blender

Why these high protein smoothie recipes work

Energy crashes come from sugar spikes, and these high protein smoothie recipes prevent them by design. Protein from nuts, seeds, and nut butters slows digestion and provides steady fuel for hours. Healthy fats add satiety so you're not hungry again at 10am. Complex carbs from oats, banana, and dates give immediate energy without the spike. The result: a glass that powers you through morning workouts, long meetings, or busy afternoons.

Each recipe is calibrated to keep you full for 3–4 hours. They're ideal as breakfast replacements or post-workout recovery. Exact quantities and smart swaps are included with every recipe.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best high protein smoothie?

Nut butter + banana + seeds is the classic — it delivers 15–20g of protein with great taste. Our Nutty Berry Prospect below is a perfect example.

Can smoothies replace protein shakes?

Yes — whole-food smoothies with nuts, seeds, and nut butter provide comparable protein with far more vitamins, fibre, and flavour than powdered shakes.

How much protein should an energy smoothie have?

Aim for 12–20g per serving for a meal replacement, 8–12g for a snack. The recipes in our Energy chapter are built to hit these targets naturally.

Are energy smoothies good before workouts?

Yes — drink one 60–90 minutes before exercise. The carbs provide immediate fuel and the protein supports muscle recovery afterwards.
