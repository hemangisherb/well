Frequently asked questions

What can I add to a smoothie for protein?

Nut butters, hemp seeds, chia seeds, oats, cacao and Greek-style plant yoghurts all lift the protein content without powder. Our 14 high protein smoothie recipes layer several of these in every glass.

Are high protein smoothies good for breakfast?

They are one of the best breakfasts: protein plus fibre keeps blood sugar steady and hunger away until lunch. Any of our high protein smoothie recipes makes a complete, five-minute morning meal.

Can I build muscle with smoothies?

Smoothies help by delivering protein and calories around training, but muscle still needs progressive exercise and enough total daily protein. Think of these high protein smoothie recipes as tasty reinforcement for your training — like Rocket in My Smoothie after leg day.
